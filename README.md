# optics_playground

Interactive webpages for exploring basic ray- and wave-optics concepts, designed for Optics in *Foundations of Physics* at the University of Bath.

## Current activities
- Lens: build arbitrary convex / concave / plano surface combinations
- Telescope: placeholder tab for the next activity
- Refraction / Snell's law
- Critical angle
- Total internal reflection

- Curved Mirror: interactive concave/convex spherical-mirror ray diagram

- Microscope: fixed 10× objective, fixed 5× eyepiece, human eye lens, and retina with adjustable object distance

- Telescope: Keplerian telescope with adjustable objective–eyepiece separation, focal-point markers, human eye lens and retina

- Aberrations: spherical, chromatic, coma, and astigmatism with ideal/real-lens comparison

- Astigmatism: dynamic screen-position slider morphs the image cross-section continuously from T to S

- Chromatic aberration (white light): inset now shows the spot sizes at the red focal plane (red sharpest, green larger, blue largest)

- Chromatic white-light inset: transverse blur radii visually magnified for clarity while focal positions/metrics remain physical

- Distortion: dynamic barrel/pincushion grid with field-angle and distortion-strength controls
